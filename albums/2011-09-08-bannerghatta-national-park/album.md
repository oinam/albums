[Bannerghatta National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bannerghatta_National_Park) is a national park located in Bangalore, Karnataka. It was founded in 1970 and declared as a national park in 1974. In 2002, a small portion of the park became a zoological garden, the Bannerghatta Biological Park.
