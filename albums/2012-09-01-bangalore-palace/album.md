[Bangalore Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengaluru_Palace) is a 19th-century royal palace located in Bangalore, Karnataka, India. It was commissioned by the Maharaja of Mysore, and was completed in 1874. The palace is open for public viewing, and in the recent years, the palace grounds have been to host various events and fairs, and is home to an amusement park.
