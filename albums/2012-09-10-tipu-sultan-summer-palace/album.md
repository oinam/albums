[Tipu Sultan’s Summer Palace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tipu_Sultan%27s_Summer_Palace), in Bangalore, India, is an example of Indo-Islamic architecture and was the summer residence of the Mysorean ruler [Tipu Sultan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tipu_Sultan). Today the Archaeological Survey of India maintains the palace, which is located at the center of Old Bangalore near the Kalasipalya bus stand, as a tourist spot.
