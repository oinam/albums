## What that means in practice

Fork the repository, run it, change it, publish your own album with it, sell what
you build on it — that is what the MIT licence is for, and no permission beyond it
is needed.

If you want the example content gone, delete `albums/` and stage your own originals
in `_incoming/`. Nothing in the build depends on the example albums existing.

If you want to use one of the photographs for something, ask. Contact details are
on the site.
